Frequently asked questions

What does Contact0 do?

Contact0 lets you create reusable message templates and keep a history of messages you've sent, so reaching out to people is faster and more consistent.

Where is my data stored?

Your message templates and history are stored on your device. They can be backed up to your own cloud account (iCloud on iOS, Google Drive / Android Auto Backup on Android). Cyborgcom does not collect, receive, or store any of your data.

How do I restore my templates and history on a new device?

Sign in to the same cloud account you used previously (iCloud on iOS or your Google account on Android), reinstall Contact0, and your backed‑up data will be restored by the operating system.

How do I disable cloud backups?

  • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud → toggle off Contact0.
  • Android: Settings → System → Backup → manage app backup settings.

The app crashes or behaves unexpectedly. What should I do?

  1. Make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store / Play Store.
  2. Restart the app, then your device.
  3. If the issue continues, email us with the details listed above.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email cyborgcomsmart@gmail.com with "Bug" or "Feature request" in the subject line.
